This timeline begins the Victorian period at 1832, the year of the first Reform Act, and ends it in 1901, when Queen Victoria died. Victoria's reign itself began in 1837. The two starting dates mark different ways of organising literary history, so it is useful to state which one a course uses.

## Novel, poetry and society

Charles Dickens used serial fiction to bring social conflict, comedy and memorable characters to a wide readership. Alfred, Lord Tennyson explored grief, belief and public duty in poetry. Thomas Hardy's novels examined rural life and the pressures of social convention near the century's end. The Brontes, George Eliot and Elizabeth Gaskell show how varied Victorian fiction was beyond any three names on a timeline card.

## Questions to ask

Industrialisation, reform, empire, scientific debate and religious doubt all shaped the period, but no single theme explains every work. Ask how a text reached readers and whose experience it represents. The [full Victorian guide](/notes/victorian-age/) covers the longer history, major genres and writers.
