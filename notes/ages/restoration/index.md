The Restoration begins with the return of Charles II in 1660. Theatres reopened after the closure of the Civil War years, and the stage became a visible part of urban literary life. The age also saw fierce political and religious debate in print.

## Theatre and satire

William Wycherley and William Congreve wrote comedies of manners about fashionable society, reputation and marriage. Aphra Behn was a major professional writer of plays and prose; *Oroonoko* is among her best-known works. John Dryden wrote across genres, from plays and criticism to political satire, including *Absalom and Achitophel*.

## What makes it distinct

Restoration drama is not simply a return to the Elizabethan theatre. Audiences, stage conventions and the place of women in performance changed. In exam answers, distinguish the 1660–1700 Restoration years from the longer Neoclassical period and the later Augustan age.
