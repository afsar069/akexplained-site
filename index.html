Meet the Author

My name is Afsar Khan. I teach English Literature, I hold a Ph.D. in the subject, and I have spent my working life in a classroom explaining the same texts to students who arrive with very different levels of preparation.

AK Explained began as a YouTube channel, on a simple and slightly stubborn idea: if a student can follow a story in a Hindi film, that student can follow Doctor Faustus. This website is the written half of the same work — notes, syllabus maps and quizzes for the three days before an examination.
