Literary Criticism

For more than two thousand years, thinkers have asked what literature is, what it is for and how it should be read. These notes gather their answers — the history of criticism, the main schools of theory, and the terms you need for your exams.

How this part is arranged

Criticism is a wide field, so it is split into sections. The first section, the history of literary criticism, follows the subject in time, from the Greeks to about 1960. The later sections group the modern schools by what they look at — the text itself, or society and power — and a final section explains the key terms. Sections open as each one is written.
