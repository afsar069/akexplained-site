Literary Criticism · Notes

Literary Theory

Theory asks the questions behind every reading. Where does meaning sit — in the words, in the writer, in the reader, or in the society that made all three? These notes answer in eight parts, one line of thought at a time.

How this series is arranged

The eight parts below run in a rough order of time and of argument: each part answers, or quarrels with, the one before it. Inside a part, every chapter is one article. An article opens with the thinkers of that school, and each name leads to a page of its own with that thinker's life, works and key ideas.
