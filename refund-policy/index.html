Refund and Cancellation Policy

When money paid for the All Access pass is returned, and when it is not.

The short version

The pass opens your mock tests the moment the payment is complete. Once you have opened a paid test, you can read and print it, so a payment is not refunded after that. If you have not opened any paid test, you can ask for a full refund within 24 hours of paying. A failed payment, money taken without a pass, and a double payment are always returned.

When you get your money back

  • You have not opened any paid test. Ask within 24 hours of your payment and you get 100% of it back. The pass then stops.
  • The payment failed, or money was taken but you did not get the pass. The full amount is returned.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase. The extra payment is returned in full.

When there is no refund

  • After you have opened a paid test. A paid test can be read and printed as soon as it opens, so the payment cannot be returned after that.
  • After 24 hours, except for a failed, wrongly taken or double payment.

The site records the first time each account opens each paid test, so this can be checked fairly.

How to ask

Write from the Contact page, using the email address of your account. Give the date of the payment and, if you have it, the payment id from Razorpay’s receipt.

When a refund is approved, it is sent within 72 hours, to the same card, UPI account or bank account you paid from. After that, the time your bank takes to show it can vary.

Cancelling

There is no automatic renewal: a pass simply ends when its days are over, and you are never charged again unless you buy again. There is no cancellation charge.
